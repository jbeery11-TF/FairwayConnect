# Week 1 Content Pack — Phase 1 "Earn Attention"

Everything you need to batch Week 1: 5 Reels and 2 carousels, plus Stories. Each piece has the hook, a shot list (phone and Google Flow), on-screen text, the caption, and hashtags. It follows [the roadmap](../SOCIAL_GROWTH_ROADMAP.md).

**Phase 1 rule:** no app talk in any video this week. The bio and handle do the branding.

**Flow style suffix** (paste at the end of every Flow prompt):
```
vertical 9:16, golden-hour light, shallow depth of field, cinematic handheld
feel, muted greens, no faces visible, no text.
```

**Hashtags:** 3–5 per post, rotated. Pick from: `#golf #golflife #golfhumor #golfswing #golfer #golfmemes #golfcourse #weekendgolfer #golfbuddies #golfislife`
