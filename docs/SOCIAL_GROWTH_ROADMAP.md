# FairwayConnect — 90-Day Faceless Social Growth Roadmap

**Goal:** 0 → 1,000 followers on Instagram in 90 days, using faceless short-form video (Google Flow + phone footage), cross-posted to YouTube Shorts and TikTok. The account then becomes the launch channel for the FairwayConnect app.

**Primary platform:** Instagram Reels. **Mirrors:** YouTube Shorts, TikTok (same vertical file, posted natively to each — don't upload with another app's watermark).

## 1. The "post golf content first, pivot later" plan — revised

Your instinct is right: build a golf audience *before* you sell the app. People follow for entertainment, not ads. Change one part of it, though: **don't delete everything on day 14 or 30.**

Why not wipe:
- The people who followed for golf humor and ripping-drive clips are exactly who FairwayConnect is for. Deleting the posts they followed for gives them no reason to stay.
- Deleting a lot of posts at once wipes out what the algorithm has learned about who to show your account to. You'd be starting over.
- It isn't needed. If you name the account FairwayConnect on day 1, the golf content is already building the brand.

**What to do instead: a soft reveal in three phases.**

| Phase | Days | What you post | Brand presence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Earn attention** | 1–21 | 100% golf entertainment: relatable humor, ripping drives, satisfying shots, course moments | Handle + name are FairwayConnect. Bio: "The golf network. Coming soon." No app talk in videos. |
| **2. Plant the flag** | 22–60 | 80% golf entertainment / 20% "the golf network" stories (finding a foursome, playing solo, golf-buddy matchmaking) | Waitlist link in bio. The app shows up at the end of 1 in 5 videos, never in the hook. |
| **3. Launch mode** | 61–90 | 60% entertainment / 25% community / 15% product (features, build-in-public, beta invites) | App/TestFlight/waitlist CTA in bio and pinned Reel. |

**For the impatience:** day 15 is your "reveal" checkpoint. Put the waitlist link in your bio and pin a "what is FairwayConnect" Reel then, but **keep the content**. If a post flops or doesn't fit, **Archive** it (Instagram's ⋯ → Archive). That hides it without deleting it.

Friends: you don't need to block anyone. Just don't count on their likes — a following made of friends who don't golf skews your early signals. Don't ask non-golfers to engage.

## 3. Faceless Production System (Google Flow + phone)

**The mix:** ~50% real phone footage (hands, clubs, ball POV, tee box, cart), ~50% Google Flow. Real clips make the account believable. Flow covers what you can't film: dream courses, exaggerated scenarios, cinematic B-roll.

**Shot types that never show a face:**
- POV over the ball, gloved hands gripping the club, feet at address
- Low ground-level shots: ball on tee → club → impact → ball flight
- Over-the-shoulder from behind, wide enough that no one can be identified
- Cart dashboard, scorecard on the steering wheel, phone screen over a green
- AI-generated golfers shot from behind, silhouetted, or very wide

**Reusable Flow prompt templates** (keep the style suffix the same every time so the feed looks consistent):

```
STYLE SUFFIX: vertical 9:16, golden-hour light, shallow depth of field,
cinematic handheld feel, muted greens, no faces visible, no text.

1. Ripping drive:  Low-angle shot from behind a golfer on an elevated tee box,
   driver swings through, ball launches into a cloudless sky over a coastal
   fairway. [STYLE SUFFIX]
2. Dream hole:    Slow drone flyover of a par-3 island green at sunrise,
   mist on the water, a single ball resting near the pin. [STYLE SUFFIX]
3. Relatable:     A golf ball sits buried in a greenside bunker, camera slowly
   pushes in, a rake lies abandoned nearby, comedic stillness. [STYLE SUFFIX]
4. Network:       Four golf bags lined up on a cart path at dawn, three
   matching and one completely different, camera dollies past them. [STYLE SUFFIX]
```

**Edit stack:** Flow clips + phone clips → CapCut (or Instagram's Edits app) → on-screen hook text in the first frame → trending or original audio → captions burned in (most people watch muted).

**AI disclosure:** Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube all require you to label realistic AI-generated video. Turn on the "AI-generated" / "altered content" label when posting Flow footage. It doesn't hurt reach the way getting caught does.

**Weekly batch (fits 6–8 hrs):**
| When | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Pick 7 hooks from the bank, write scripts, generate Flow clips | 2.5 hrs |
| On the course (any day) | Shoot 15–20 phone clips | 0.5 hr (while playing) |
| Monday | Edit 5 Reels + 2 carousels, schedule them all in Meta Business Suite | 2.5 hrs |
| Daily | Reply to comments, 10 min of commenting on bigger golf accounts | 15 min/day |

**Post at:** 6–8am (pre-round scrollers) or 7–9pm. Check Insights after week 3 and use your own audience's peak times.

## 5. Post Formats

1. **Text-hook Reel:** a bold one-liner over B-roll. The main format, the easiest to batch.
2. **POV Reel:** "POV: …" in first person, with a phone or Flow clip.
3. **"Every foursome has…" series:** a recurring series, one archetype per episode (the sandbagger, the range-finder guy, the beer-cart loyalist). Series bring people back.
4. **Satisfying loop:** impact + ball flight + sound, no talking, perfect loop.
5. **Carousel meme / list:** "5 types of golfers you meet as a single". Gets saved and shared in DMs.
6. **Golf Résumé template (P3):** "Fill out your golf résumé: Handicap / Home course / Worst club / Golf crime". People fill it out in the comments. It's an engagement magnet and it previews the app's profile feature.
7. **Build-in-public (P4):** a screen recording of the app plus a text overlay: "Day 47 building a LinkedIn for golfers".

## 7. Monthly Milestones & What to Track

| Checkpoint | Followers | Other targets | If you're behind |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 15 (reveal)** | 50–100 | 1 Reel over 1k views; bio link live | Shorter Reels, stronger first-frame text |
| **Day 30** | 150–250 | 20+ posts live; 1 Reel over 5k views; know your top pillar | Double down on the best pillar, cut the worst |
| **Day 60** | 400–550 | 1 Reel over 20k views; 50+ waitlist signups; one series running | Collab with 2–3 small golf accounts (5–50k followers) |
| **Day 90** | **1,000+** | 150+ waitlist/beta signups; 2+ follows per 1k views | Review the top 10 posts and rebuild the plan around them |

**Weekly scorecard** (10 minutes every Sunday, in a spreadsheet):

| Metric | Why it matters | Healthy target |
|---|---|---|
| Avg. views per Reel | Reach | Rising week over week |
| Shares per 1k views | The algorithm's strongest signal | 5+ |
| Saves per 1k views | How valuable the content is | 3+ |
| Follows per 1k views | Whether people convert to followers | 2–5 |
| 3-sec hold rate (Insights → Reel) | Whether the hook works | 60%+ |
| Profile visits → link clicks | Whether the app story lands | Track from Day 15 |
| Waitlist signups | The real business goal | Track from Day 15 |

**Rule of thumb:** high views with low follows means the content isn't tied to an identity, so lean on series and the FairwayConnect angle. Low views means the hook failed. Fix the first second before anything else.

## 8. Growth Accelerators (beyond posting)

- **Comment strategy:** 10 minutes a day leaving genuinely funny comments on big golf accounts. Faceless accounts grow fastest this way.
- **Collabs (Day 45+):** Instagram's Collab post feature with small golf creators. Offer them free beta "Founding Member" profiles.
- **Golf-course angle:** tag the course in real-footage posts. Local courses often repost.
- **Community prompts:** "Drop your handicap + home course." That's real users for the app showing up in your comments.
- **Cross-post:** the same file goes to YouTube Shorts and TikTok within 24 hours. Shorts also feeds the long-term faceless YouTube channel. Once the Shorts work, compile the best ones into 5–8 minute videos ("Every Type of Golfer, Ranked").
